## 3. EQUITY STRUCTURE

### 3.0 Two-Pool Structure

The company equity is divided into two pools:

| Pool | % of Total Company | Description |
|------|-------------------|-------------|
| **Investor Pool** | 60-80% | External capital partner(s) |
| **Founder Pool** | 20-40% | Split between JI and AZ |

**IMPORTANT:** AZ and JI equity percentages below refer to their share of the **Founder Pool**, not the total company.

### 3.1 Founder Pool Split

| Founder | Min % of Founder Pool | Max % of Founder Pool |
|---------|----------------------|----------------------|
| **JI (Medical Director)** | 80% | 98% |
| **AZ (Arturs Zalitis)** | 2% | 20% |

### 3.2 Arturs Zalitis (AZ) - Two-Step Equity Structure

**STEP 1: 2% of Founder Pool**
*Trigger: Investment approved (before project execution)*

AZ receives 2% of the Founder Pool when:
- Lead investor signs term sheet / investment is approved
- AZ was heavily involved in:
  - Documentation and data room preparation, AND/OR
  - Investor sourcing and negotiations

**STEP 2: 20% of Founder Pool**
*Trigger: 12 months after launch with good performance in AZ's scope of responsibility*

**Key Principle:** AZ is evaluated ONLY on items within AZ's direct control. Medical/clinical items are JI's responsibility.

## 5. GOVERNANCE

### 5.1 Decision Rights Matrix

| Decision Type | AZ | JI | Investor | Required |
|--------------|-----|-----|----------|----------|
| Day-to-day operations | ✓ | ✓ | - | Either founder |
| Hiring staff (< €50K/yr) | ✓ | ✓ | - | Either founder |
| Hiring executives | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | Unanimous |
| Budget > €50K | - | ✓ | ✓ | Investor + JI |
| Clinical protocols | - | ✓ | - | JI only |
| Property acquisition | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | Unanimous |
| New equity issuance | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | Unanimous |
| Sale of company | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | Unanimous |
| Debt financing | - | ✓ | ✓ | Investor + JI |

### 5.2 Board Structure
- **Board Size:** 3-5 members
- **Composition:**
  - 1-2 Investor representatives
  - 1 JI (Medical Director)
  - 1 AZ (if 20% equity achieved)
  - 1 Independent (optional)

### 5.3 Reporting Requirements
- Monthly: Operating metrics, cash position
- Quarterly: Full P&L, balance sheet, KPIs
- Annually: Audited financials, strategic review

## 6. EXIT PROVISIONS

### 6.1 Exit Timeline
- **Lock-up Period:** 3 years (no exits allowed)
- **Exit Window:** Years 4-7
- **Target Exit:** Year 5-7 via strategic sale or investor buyout

### 6.2 Exit Mechanisms
1. **Strategic Sale:** Sale to hospitality group, wellness chain, or PE fund
2. **Investor Buyout:** Investor purchases founder shares at fair market value
3. **Founder Buyout:** Founders purchase investor shares (unlikely given capital constraints)
4. **IPO:** Not anticipated for this project size

### 6.3 Tag-Along / Drag-Along
- **Tag-Along:** Founders can sell proportionally if investor sells
- **Drag-Along:** If 75%+ shareholders agree to sale, all must sell
